What is OFW Simulator?

The Optical Fibre Simulator is a web-based tool that helps users understand how data is transmitted through optical fibers using light signals. It allows users to explore concepts like signal loss, fiber type, and wavelength through simple and interactive visualization.

WORKING PRINCIPLE

  • User provides inputs such as fiber type, length, wavelength, core refractive index (n₁), cladding refractive index (n₂), and incident angle (θ).
  • The simulator checks the basic condition n₁ > n₂ for light propagation.
  • If n₁ ≤ n₂, light cannot stay inside the fiber and escapes due to refraction.
  • If n₁ > n₂, the simulator proceeds to calculate the critical angle (θc).
  • The critical angle is calculated using the formula θc = sin⁻¹(n₂/n₁).
  • The incident angle (θ) is compared with the critical angle (θc).
  • If θ > θc, Total Internal Reflection (TIR) occurs.
  • If θ ≤ θc, refraction occurs and light escapes from the fiber.
  • When TIR occurs, light travels through the fiber by reflecting internally.
  • During transmission, signal loss (attenuation) is calculated based on fiber length.
  • The simulator determines the output signal strength after loss.
  • Finally, the result is displayed showing whether TIR or refraction occurs along with signal loss.

OBJECTIVES

1. To simulate the working principle of optical fibre communication in an interactive way.

2. To understand how light is transmitted through an optical fibre using the concept of total internal reflection.

3. To analyze how refractive indices (n₁ and n₂) affect signal propagation in the fibre.

4. To calculate critical angle and observe conditions for signal transmission and loss.

5. To demonstrate signal attenuation and data loss in optical communication systems.

6. To provide a visual and educational tool for students to learn fibre optics easily.

7. To enhance practical understanding of theoretical physics concepts through simulation.
